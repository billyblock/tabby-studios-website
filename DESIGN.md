# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World

Warm editorial, neutral-first: paper/cream/ink carry the page, and marmalade orange (`--marmalade`) is a restrained accent — links, small badges, the check-icon circles, a single accent button, thin decorative marks — never a full-bleed section background or a large filled card on the homepage. Text on any orange fill is dark ink (`--ink`), never white — white fails contrast at the sizes used here. No pictorial logo or icon mark anywhere in the UI — identity is carried by the `Wordmark` component, a single-line serif lockup ("Tabby" in ink, "Studios" in clay), not a multi-part logotype. `app/icon.svg` is the browser-tab favicon only, not a brand mark used elsewhere. The name is cat-themed, but no cat iconography is used anywhere — identity stays typographic, not pictorial. This "no imagery" rule is about logo/icon marks specifically, not photography: `/about` uses a real photo of the founder (`public/headshot-outside.png`, in a rounded-`--radius-lg` frame with a tinted shadow, not a circular avatar) — that's the one legitimate photo on the site, since it's evidence of the solo-operator claim, not a brand mark. The photo displays at its native ~3:2 ratio (`quality={95}` on `next/image`, `max-width: 620px`) rather than a portrait crop, specifically to avoid asking the browser to upscale it past what its source resolution (1264×844) can render sharply on retina displays.

**Living accents**: every page hero (home, pricing, FAQ, contact, thank-you) carries one soft, slow-drifting organic blob (`--marmalade-soft`, `border-radius` keyframe-animated, `blobDrift`) tucked behind the heading — this is the site's one recurring "alive" signature, restrained to a single blob per non-homepage hero (the homepage keeps its original two). Each hero's CSS module defines its own local `@keyframes blobDrift`; CSS Modules scope keyframe names per file, so this cannot be centralized into `globals.css` without breaking the `animation` reference — duplicate the block rather than trying to share it.

The pricing and FAQ pages' CTA bands and the pricing page's recommended-tier card match the homepage's restrained treatment: ink (`var(--ink)`) fills with orange used only for price text, badges, and small accents — never a full orange fill with white text.

## Typography

- Headings: Fraunces (serif, weights 400–700), tight tracking on large sizes.
- Body/UI: Work Sans (sans, weights 400–800).
- No eyebrow/kicker labels above headings anywhere — this is a hard rule, not a default; headings carry their own weight. If a category label is genuinely needed, fold it into the heading or body copy instead.

## Color tokens (`app/globals.css`)

`--ink` (#201c17, primary text/dark surfaces), `--ink-soft`, `--text-soft` (muted body), `--marmalade` (#dc7f31, the accent orange — links, small fills, badges), `--marmalade-deep` (darker orange for hover states), `--clay` (#9c4f22, deep orange-brown for link/price text on light backgrounds), `--marmalade-soft` (pale tint for check-icon circles), `--cream` (muted section background), `--paper` (base background), `--rule`/`--rule-strong` (hairline borders), `--shadow` (tinted, low-opacity — never pure black).

## Components

- **Buttons**: `--radius-btn` (6px) everywhere, including the homepage's primary CTAs — never pill-shaped, never sharp/0. Background-color shift on hover, `scale(0.98)` on `:active` — no lift/shadow theatrics.
- **Small tags/badges** (recommended-tier badge): pill-shaped is fine here — this is the one place `border-radius: 999px` is earned.
- **Never same-size cards**: repeated content avoids the grid-of-equal-boxes pattern. The pricing page's "getting started" steps render as a connected timeline with an index number (earned back since the sequence is real information), not a card grid.
- **Pricing tiers**: the one legitimate 3-up card grid on the homepage (a real comparison, not a lazy content container). Recommended tier is an ink-filled surface with an orange price and orange badge — color as a small pop, not a full fill.
- **FAQ accordion**: no card box around each item — hairline `border-bottom` only, plain `+`/`−` marker (CSS-drawn cross that collapses to a dash on open, `app/faq/FaqItem.js`). Built as a controlled `button` + `role="region"` panel animated with the `grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr` technique (same one `MobileNav` uses), not native `<details>` — native details/summary snaps open/closed with no transition.
- **Hero markup structure**: every hero is `<section><div className="wrapper"><div className={styles.heroInner}>...</div></div></section>` — `wrapper` (max-width 1200, `margin: 0 auto`) and the page's own narrower `heroInner` (max-width-narrow, 720) must be on two *separate* nested divs, never combined as `` `wrapper ${styles.heroInner}` `` on one element. Combining them left `wrapper`'s centering margin active even after `heroInner`'s narrower max-width won the cascade, silently shifting every hero ~280px off the left edge on any viewport wider than 1200px — a real bug that shipped for a while, not a style choice. Every hero (about, faq, contact, contact/thank-you, start, start/confirmed, pricing, privacy, terms) uses the two-div structure now; the homepage hero is exempt since it's intentionally centered via `text-align: center` on its own still-separate `.heroCopy` div, not this pattern.
- **Sections are flush-left by default** (constrained max-width, no `margin: auto`): pricing, contact, start, privacy, terms all follow this. **About and FAQ are the deliberate exception** — both are centered end to end (the FAQ hero via `margin: 0 auto` + `text-align: center`; About's content column below its hero, and FAQ's category blocks, via `margin: 0 auto` alone) per an explicit user call that flush-left didn't suit a personal-intro page (About) or a Q&A page (FAQ). Inside that centered column, prose paragraphs and the FAQ accordion rows stay left-aligned (only the column itself centers, not multi-line body text) — centering running text produces ragged, harder-to-read edges. Don't flush-left these two pages again without being asked; don't centered-column any of the other pages without being asked either.
- **About hero is the one asymmetric two-column layout on the site**: name/heading/subtitle on the left (`1.3fr`, no eyebrow label above the heading — that's still a hard no per Typography above), photo (portrait `3/4`, fills its `0.7fr` grid column) on the right, bottom-aligned (`align-items: end`), stacking to a small centered circle-ish photo above centered text under 640px. This is a specific, requested exception to the centered treatment directly above — the hero splits asymmetrically, only the *content column below it* centers. `object-position: 44% 22%` on the photo isn't arbitrary: the source photo's subject isn't horizontally centered in the original frame, so the default `50%` crop visibly pushed the face left inside the narrower `3:4` box — 44% was solved empirically (screenshot, measure, adjust) to actually center the face, not just the frame. If the source photo ever changes, re-derive this value rather than assuming 44% still applies. Separately: this Next.js version (16) requires `images.qualities` to be explicitly set in `next.config.mjs` — it defaults to `[75]` only, and any `quality={n}` prop on `next/image` silently clamps to the nearest allowed value instead of erroring, so `quality={95}` on the photo was doing nothing (serving at 75) until `qualities: [75, 95]` was added to the config. If another image is ever added with a different quality prop, add that value to the allowlist too or it'll silently clamp the same way.
- **Cards go warm on hover**: pricing tier cards (both the homepage teaser and the full pricing page) lift slightly with a tinted shadow and a marmalade border on hover — no lift/shadow on buttons (see above), but cards are a distinct category and this is the one place a lift is earned.
- **Scroll-reveal is opt-in and sequence-only**: `components/Reveal.js` + the global `.revealGroup` utility staggers a list's direct children in on scroll. Reserved for lists where order is real information — the pricing page's numbered "getting started" steps, and each FAQ category's question list — never applied as a generic "everything fades in" treatment. Content is visible by default and only primes to hidden after the client script actually mounts, so a blocked or failed script never hides it.
- **Nav shows the current page**: header and mobile nav links get an active state (`--clay` text plus a thin `--marmalade` underline on desktop) when the link matches the current route, via `usePathname()`.
- **Tier picker (`/start`)**: real `<input type="radio">` elements (visually hidden, not `display: none`, so they stay focusable) wrapped in a `<label>`, with the visible card styled via `:has(input:checked)` — not a JS-driven fake selector. Selected state is a marmalade border + `--marmalade-soft` fill, matching the check-icon/badge accent language rather than inventing a new selected-state color.

## Homepage: deliberately three sections, not six

The homepage is hero → pricing teaser → closing CTA band. It previously also carried an "about" section (two paragraphs + a pull-quote panel), a services list (four paragraphs explaining mobile/SEO/pricing/one-person), and a process timeline (four steps) — all removed on request to cut everything a visitor doesn't need explained before they can grasp "this business builds websites" and act (Get Started / See pricing). The four one-line trust claims that used to be paragraphs now live as a compact checklist row (`.essentials`) under the hero's CTAs. Don't reintroduce the longer explanatory sections, the pull-quote, or a "scroll to learn more" cue (it invites reading instead of acting) without being asked — the detail those sections carried (process, differentiators, philosophy) still exists on `/pricing` and `/faq` for visitors who want it.

## What not to reintroduce

No kicker/eyebrow text labels, no pill-shaped primary buttons or large containers, no flat linear-gradient section backgrounds (a low-opacity radial ambient glow behind the homepage hero is the one permitted exception), no pure-black untinted box-shadows, no white text set on an orange fill (contrast fails — use ink), no logo/icon imagery in the header, footer, hero, or anywhere else in the page UI, and no full-bleed orange section backgrounds on the homepage without being asked again.
